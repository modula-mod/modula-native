## MOL-03A deliverables

* `ios/Modula/Resources/Moliboo/moliboo_registry.json` — schema `modula.moliboo.registry.1`, 115 characters
  (`moliboo.001`…`moliboo.115`), 7 families, 7 motion profiles, the expression contract and the 11 animation primitives.
  Generated by `Tools/moliboo/registry/generate_registry.py` from the roster in code (names, numbers, titles, traits and
  forms are never renamed); personality numbers and signature reactions are derived deterministically from each
  character's own traits and rarest silhouette part.
* `MolibooRegistry.swift` (`MolibooCharacterRegistry`) — Codable model, lazy bundled loader, `MolibooRegistryValidator` (schema, unique ids/numbers/
  keys/packages, family ranges, contiguous numbering, wearable ↔ family, fit-profile resolution, expressions,
  reactions, motion profiles, Reduce Motion rules, personality ranges).
* Validation runs in CI (`runtime/tests/moliboo-registry.test.ts`, including a generator `--check` so the JSON cannot drift
  from code) and in `MOL03RegistryTests` (registry ↔ native roster, saved selections, negative cases, extensibility
  beyond 115).

Personality metadata is behavioural styling only; it does not imply real emotions or awareness.
